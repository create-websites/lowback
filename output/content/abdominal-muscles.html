More Than Just a Six-Pack.

The core is not a shield; it is a pressurized cylinder. Its true purpose is not to create motion (crunches), but to resist motion (stability) and transfer energy from hips to hands.

The 3 Myths of Core Training

  • !

    Myth: "Crunches burn belly fat."
    Reality: Spot reduction is impossible. Crunches flex the spine under load.

  • !

    Myth: "Suck it in."
    Reality: Stability comes from pushing out (bracing) to create pressure, not hollowing.

  • ✓

    Truth: "Anti-Movement."
    A strong core prevents the spine from buckling under heavy loads.

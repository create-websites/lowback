Pain Management Strategies

Here's a rewrite of the line, keeping a similar size and focus: For mild knee pain from strain or overuse, home remedies often help a lot. Understanding which treatments to use, and when, is vital for success.

❄️ RICE Protocol (First 48 Hours)

Best for acute injuries & inflammation.

  • Rest: Avoid activities that cause pain.
  • Ice: Apply for 15-20 min every 2-3 hours to reduce swelling.
  • Compression: Use an elastic bandage to limit swelling.
  • Elevation: Keep the knee raised above heart level.

🔥 Heat Therapy (After 48 Hours)

Best for chronic stiffness & muscle soreness.

For muscle relaxation and better blood flow, try a heating pad or warm compress for 15-20 minutes before exercising to reduce stiffness.

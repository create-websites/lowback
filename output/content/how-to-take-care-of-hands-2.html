Ergonomics & Prevention

To avoid hand/wrist pain, reduce repetitive strain. Good ergonomics at your computer and with tools are key to preventing long-term injury.

💻 Computer Workstation

  • Neutral Wrists: Maintain straight wrists; avoid bending them upward, downward, or sideways.
  • Elbows at 90°: Here are a few options, all similar in length: * **Set chair height: aim for 90-degree elbow bends.** * **Angle elbows: adjust chair for a right angle.** * **Chair height: find a 90-degree elbow bend.** * **Adjust chair until elbows form right angles.**
  • Monitor at Eye Level: Here are a few options, all similar in length: * Place the screen's top near or just under your eyes. * Set the monitor's top at or a bit below eye height. * Ensure screen's top is level with or lower than your eyes. * The screen's top: eye level or slightly beneath.
  • Take Micro-Breaks: Every 10-15 minutes, let your hands relax and stretch.

🛠️ Hand Tool Use

  • Power Grip: Employ your entire hand to grasp tools, rather than just your digits.
  • Proper Fit: Use tools with handles that fit your hand comfortably.
  • Avoid Vibration: Use anti-vibration gloves or materials for vibrating tools.
  • Vary Tasks: Alternate between different tasks to avoid repetitive motions.

Holistic Health Factors

Our overall well-being strongly influences the musculoskeletal system, particularly the hands. Maintaining a healthy weight, diet, and sleep schedule is crucial for reducing inflammation and promoting tissue repair.

⚖️

Weight Management

Obesity elevates systemic inflammation, potentially exacerbating arthritis, including in non-weight-bearing joints such as the hands.

Optimal Sleep

Prioritize quality sleep to aid muscle recovery and inflammation reduction. Employ pillows for neutral wrist positioning, minimizing discomfort.

Stop Stretching. Start Stabilizing.

The lower back is designed to be a stiff pillar that transmits power, not a flexible willow branch. Pain occurs when we force the stable lumbar spine to do the mobile work of the hips.

The 3 Myths of Back Pain

  • ✕

    Myth: "I need to stretch my back."
    Reality: Stretching often triggers the protective stretch reflex, increasing pain.

  • ✕

    Myth: "Sit-ups strengthen the core."
    Reality: They place massive compressive loads on the discs.

  • ✓

    Truth: "Proximal stiffness for distal athleticism."
    A stiff core allows the arms and legs to move powerfully.

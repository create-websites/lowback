Immediate Relief Strategies

For sudden back pain, these easy self-care tips can ease discomfort and aid healing. Discover the best times for heat or cold and explore suitable OTC treatments.

❄️ Cold Therapy

Best for: First 48-72 hours (Acute Injury)

Apply a towel-wrapped ice pack to reduce inflammation, swelling, and pain for up to 20 minutes.

🔥 Heat Therapy

Best for: After 72 hours (Ongoing Stiffness)

Soothes muscles, boosts circulation, and enhances suppleness. Use a heating pad/warm bath for 15-20 mins.

When to Seek Immediate Medical Attention

While most back pain eases on its own, certain "red flag" symptoms suggest a serious issue needing prompt medical attention. If these arise, seek immediate medical care.

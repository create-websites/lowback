What Causes Knee Pain?

Knee pain affects many people, arising from diverse causes. Injuries are common triggers, but repetitive strain and age-related joint deterioration contribute significantly to the problem.

Your Proactive Playbook for Prevention

Preventing knee pain is the best approach. Consistent, healthy routines create injury resistance. Strong knee muscles offer stability and protection.

Key Lifestyle Habits

⚖️

Manage Weight

Each pound gained puts 3-5 extra pounds of stress on your knees; thus, weight management is key for knee health.

👟

Choose Proper Footwear

Opt for shoes with ample support and cushioning. Unsuitable footwear throws off alignment, stressing your knees directly.

🏋️

Lift Smart

Prioritize leg strength when lifting. Squat down, hug the weight, and avoid twisting your torso.

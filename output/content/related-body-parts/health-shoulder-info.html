Anatomy of Shoulder Pain

Shoulder pain stems from various causes. Rotator cuff overuse is typical, with arthritis following closely. Trauma and pain referred from elsewhere occur less often, yet are significant factors.

Prevention & Daily Habits

To keep shoulders healthy, integrate mindful practices into your day. Proper posture, setup of workspaces, and careful lifting techniques are key to preventing strain.

🧍 Conscious Posture

Maintain good posture: sit and stand straight, drawing shoulders back and down. Prevent slouching to avoid muscle imbalances and rotator cuff issues.

💼 Ergonomic Workspace

Position chair/screen: 90° elbows, relaxed shoulders, eye-level screen. Stretch every half hour.

🏋️ Safe Lifting

To protect your back and shoulders, lift with your legs, keeping heavy items near your body. Avoid twisting movements during the lift.

Guided Shoulder Exercises

For shoulder health, a well-rounded exercise plan is key. It combines rotator cuff and muscle strengthening for stability with stretching to boost flexibility and ward off stiffness.

Sports Injury Report

The Agility Killer.

Groin strains don't just happen; they are the result of an imbalance. When your outer hips (glutes) overpower your inner thighs (adductors), the weakest link snaps during a cut or sprint.

The "Sporting Groin" Profile

  • !

    Change of Direction: Rapid cutting lengthens the adductor while it tries to contract. This "eccentric load" causes tears.

  • !

    Chronic Neglect: Most athletes train Glutes/Quads but ignore Adductors, creating a dangerous strength gap.

  • ✓

    The Fix: The Copenhagen Plank. One exercise proven to reduce groin injury risk by 41%.
